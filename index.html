Company introduction

A diversified Indian financial services company

According to Bajaj Finance's official About Us page, it is a subsidiary of Bajaj Finserv Ltd. and a deposit-taking NBFC registered with the Reserve Bank of India. It began as Bajaj Auto Finance Limited in 1987 and was renamed Bajaj Finance Limited in 2010.

Its products span consumer, personal, rural, MSME and commercial loans, loans against securities, and deposits. It holds a stake in Bajaj Housing Finance Ltd., which handles housing finance.

Source: bajajfinserv.in/corporate-bajaj-finance. Figures change; check the official page for current numbers.
